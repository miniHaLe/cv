Photography credits. The authoritative list also lives in the <head> of
landing.html (search "Photography, with thanks"); this file mirrors it and
adds the provenance chain back to the untouched originals.

Every frame is rendered through filter:grayscale(1), so each ships as a
grayscale WebP right-sized to the largest box it is ever drawn in — the
colour was being downloaded and then discarded.

The originals are NOT in this directory. They live in design/_source/
(repo-root relative; ../../_source/ from here), deliberately outside
design/assets/ so that deploying the asset tree does not ship 23 MB of
unused JPEG. Re-derive a frame from there if it is ever needed at a
different size.
