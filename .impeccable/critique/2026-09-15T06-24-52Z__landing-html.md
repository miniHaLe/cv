## Overall Impression

High-craft, genuinely authored page with a voice that earns trust. Three things fight it: the first three seconds (preloader, scramble, slow h1) delay the thesis past an executive's patience; the seven-card pinned reel is a long flat stretch ending six times in a locked door; and mobile chrome collides on first paint. Biggest opportunity: cut the intro and the reel to what a CTO needs in eight seconds.

## What's Working

1. **The detector vocabulary is the brand.** Favicon, cursor reticle, hero brackets, CCTV overlay, schematics, portrait marks: one idea, five expressions, zero stock iconography.
2. **Engineering honesty is designed in.** Ticker pause (WCAG 2.2.2), `inert` during preload, focus-to-scroll inside the pinned rail, HEAD-probe CV fallback, thorough reduced-motion and print paths, visible 2px red focus rings.
3. **Copy has teeth.** "Most AI work stops at the demo. Mine survives a lunch rush." / "Anyone can make a model agree with them." Reader's business language, not the engineer's.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Mobile chrome collision: dock overlaps the email pill.**
Why: at 400px `.dock` spans x 14→326 and `.hail` x 248→363 (78px overlap); `Theme` is covered, both pill borders cross. First screen on a shared phone link is broken chrome and the persistent contact CTA is unreadable.
Fix: in `@media (max-width:560px)` collapse dock to sig + Work + Contact (drop Method, move Theme to footer), or drop `.hail` below 560px since the dock already has Contact. Verify at 360/390/400/430.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P1] First three seconds work against the audience.**
Why: `#pre` "Loading type" (300–1100ms), then the name kicker scrambles ~0.9s, then h1 rises 1.15s. Name unreadable ~1.5s, no CTA until ~2s. Detector flags the same kicker as a hero eyebrow chip.
Fix: drop `#pre` (the `size-adjust` fallback face already prevents reflow); cap h1 rise at 0.6s with CTA row entering at 0.3s; reserve scramble for below-fold eyebrows, never the name.
Suggested command: /impeccable quieter

**[P1] Outcome typography breaks on the two non-numeric cards.**
Why: `.slab .out strong` is 26px display width sized for `399` / `101` / `95%`; "In commercial use" wraps 2–3 lines on card 01, the flagship. `#method` sticky h2 at 74px in a `.9fr` column breaks authored `.ln` spans into 4 lines. `GATEWAY` / `TOKEN` schematic labels lack `text-anchor="middle"` and overflow their rects.
Fix: give cards 01 and 03 a numeric lead (`<strong>120</strong> RPS at TP95 · in commercial use`), add `.slab .out strong{white-space:nowrap}`; set `.split h2{font-size:clamp(23px,4vw,58px)}` or widen to `1fr 1fr`; add `text-anchor="middle"` on `.viz text.lb`.
Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

**[P2] Pinned reel is the flattest stretch and hides the disclosure it depends on.**
Why: 7 cards, ~3,470px hijacked scroll desktop, 5,136px mobile, six ending in identical "Request repository access →" mailto. The NDA / two-business-days explanation sits in `.access-note` above the rail, off-screen once pinned. Page says "I would rather show you a repository" and then shows six locked doors.
Fix: rail carries 4 cards (01, 02, 06, 07: the ones with a verifiable number or public link), fold 03–05 into a compact "Also shipped" list; put "opens an email · NDA · 2 business days" as microcopy under each link; make card 07's GitHub link look like an open door, distinct from the requests.
Suggested command: /impeccable distill

**[P2] Decision moment lacks a face and a next step.**
Why: `design/assets/photos/portrait.jpg` is absent, so `.portrait` renders the drawn "HA" stand-in next to a real CCTV photograph; reads as unfinished at the moment of deciding to email. "Start a conversation" is a bare `mailto:` with no subject or hint.
Fix: ship the portrait (grayscale, blended like the frames); add `?subject=` scaffold to the mailto; one line under the button: "Tell me the camera and the problem. I reply within two business days."
Suggested command: /impeccable onboard
