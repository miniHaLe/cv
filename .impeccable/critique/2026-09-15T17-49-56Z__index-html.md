## Priority Issues

- **[P0] The work reel had no mouse affordance, and part of it was unreachable.**
  Measured at 1440x900: 10 horizontal wheel events moved the rail 0px; a 600px
  click-drag moved it 0px and selected body copy instead; #reel was
  overflow:hidden so there was no scrollbar; cursor computed `auto`. Under
  prefers-reduced-motion the JS returned before the pin was built, leaving a
  nowrap row inside a clipping box — card 04, the only public repository on the
  page, measured 0% visible with no mechanism to reach it. Same state if
  gsap.min.js fails to load.
  Fix: replace the pin with a native scroller. RESOLVED.

- **[P1] The stepper lied about position.** face() ran inside ScrollTrigger's
  onUpdate while a 0.8s scrub was still catching up, and never re-ran at rest.
  Measured wrong at 4 of 7 positions; pressing End left card 4 centred with
  "01" marked. Fix: derive from one number, rail.scrollLeft. RESOLVED.

- **[P1] WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name (Level A), invisible to axe.** Stepper
  buttons showed "01" and were named "TesselAI · retail shelf scoring"; three
  "Request access" links were named "Request repository access for …"; the
  GitHub link showed "Open on GitHub" and was named "Open the source on
  GitHub". A voice-control user saying what they can read activates nothing.
  ZH additionally failed on work.c1-c3 and the contact pill. RESOLVED in four
  locales.

- **[P1] Copy gave no confirmation to assistive technology.** Visible label
  swapped to "Copied"; aria-label stayed "Copy email address"; the page had
  zero live regions. RESOLVED with a polite status region.

- **[P2] No forced-colors support.** In Windows High Contrast the red fill on
  the current step collapsed to Canvas, making the current step
  indistinguishable from the other three. RESOLVED.

- **[P2] The pin was unconditional at wide viewports.** At 2560x1400 four cards
  (1,872px) already fitted, and the visitor was scroll-locked for 1,422px
  anyway. RESOLVED — the row now centres when it fits.

- **[P2] The 840px pin gate was ~28px short of its own requirement**; at exactly
  1001x840 the stepper was clipped by the viewport edge. RESOLVED — gate deleted.

- **[P3] Persistent contact CTA degrades to an unlabelled avatar at 390px.** OPEN.
