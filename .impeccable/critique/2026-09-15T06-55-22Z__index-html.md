## Priority Issues

**[P1] Fixed dock covers the pinned reel cards' headers.**
Why: at pin start (1440×900) card tops sit at y≈62 while `.dock` spans y 12–80; card 02's `.who` ("DFLO · 2025–PRESENT") and `.no` are hidden for the whole pin. The reel is the proof section and the first thing hidden is the employer.
Fix: give the pinned rail headroom: `#reel{padding-top:110px}` (desktop pinned branch only) or `.rail{padding-top:100px}` inside `(min-width:1001px) and (min-height:760px)`.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] Reel progress indicator is never visible during the reel.**
Why: `.reel-bar` and "Scroll to advance the reel" live in `.reel-head`, which is above the viewport once `#reel` pins. The pin removes the scrollbar's account of position; the element built to give it back is off-screen.
Fix: move `.reel-bar` inside `#reel` below `.rail`, `position:absolute; bottom:24px; left:30px; right:30px`, so it pins with the cards.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P2] Mobile hero hierarchy flattens.**
Why: h1 is 32px at 400 and 29px at 360; the thesis is 18px serif over 5 lines; the two stacked buttons are 166 / 181px wide; `.hero .meta` becomes 6 lines of uppercase mono with key/value structure lost. Casey's first screen is 60% body copy.
Fix: `h1{font-size:clamp(40px,10.5vw,104px)}`; at ≤560px `.cta-row .btn{width:100%; justify-content:center}`; render `.hero .meta` as a two-column `dl` (`grid-template-columns:auto 1fr`) so Now / Based / Open to stay as keys.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Close section composition at desktop.**
Why: `.close .duo{align-items:end}` pushes the lede and CTAs to the bottom of the portrait's height, leaving a ~220px hole under the h2 at 1440 (h2 ends y≈370, lede starts y≈590). The eye drops off the headline into nothing before finding the button.
Fix: `align-items:start` with `margin-top:34px` on `.say`, or move the portrait beside the h2 so the two-column split starts at the eyebrow.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P3] Mailto is the only channel; no fallback when there is no mail client.**
Why: 9 `mailto:` links, 2 of them the primary CTA. On a locked-down corporate laptop a click can do nothing, silently, at the one action the page exists for.
Fix: add a copy-email button beside `thanhha.le@outlook.com` in `.close .links` (`navigator.clipboard.writeText`, label swaps to "Copied"), and surface LinkedIn as a ghost beside "Start a conversation".
Suggested command: /impeccable harden
