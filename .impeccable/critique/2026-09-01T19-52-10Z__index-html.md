## Overall Impression

Work of someone with taste, undercut by five fixable defects — three contradicting the page's own stated principles. Hero is excellent: the medium performs the message. Then 2.4s of a 45-second recruiter session goes to a fabricated progress bar, 2000px of scroll to seven unclickable cards, and the page ends on a shattered headline.

Biggest opportunity: the work section is the one place on a page arguing "everything I claim is checkable" where a reader can check nothing. Seven cards, zero links, zero images — on a computer-vision portfolio.

## What's Working

1. **Canvas is argument, not decoration.** The hero's claim is demonstrated by the substrate it sits on, and costs the reader nothing (z-index:0, aria-hidden, pointer-events:none).
2. **Copy earns the austerity.** "Most AI work stops at the demo. Mine survives a lunch rush." A page this stripped-down collapses if the words are generic. They aren't.
3. **Light theme is a second design, not an inversion.** --line lifted #242c36 to #aca291; --grain .038 to .06; --grain-blend overlay to multiply. Wired three ways.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Closing headline breaks apart on the highest-stakes screen
`.close h2{max-width:15ch}` (:332) computes to 621px at 74px type. Two of three authored .ln boxes render at 139px = two visual lines each. Intended 3-line break becomes 5 ragged lines with "AND A" orphaned. gsap.from(yPercent:110) then slides two lines as one block.
Why: peak-end rule. Last thing a recruiter sees. Looks like a bug at the moment the page argues for rigor.
Fix: delete max-width:15ch — the .ln spans already control the break. Scope to @media (max-width:900px) if a narrow cap is wanted.
Command: /impeccable typeset

### [P1] Non-text contrast 1.38:1 on every interactive boundary
--line:#242c36 on --ground:#0b0d10 = 1.38:1; WCAG 1.4.11 requires 3:1. Borders .btn.ghost, .dock button, every .slab, underlines all four contact links.
Why: one of two actions you want has an invisible boundary; the other affected element is the whole contact block.
Fix: add --line-strong:#3b4653 (dark) / #8a8071 (light) for .btn.ghost, .dock button, .slab, .close .links a. Keep --line decorative.
Command: /impeccable audit

### [P1] Work section is a dead end
Seven .slab cards: zero links, zero images, zero repo refs except plain-text github.com/miniHaLe in card 07. Costs ~2000px of pinned scroll at 1440px.
Why: this is the evidence. A CV portfolio with no images asks the reader to take vision work on faith.
Fix: wrap each .slab in an <a> to repo/case study/anchor. Add one real inference frame per vision project. Replace the pin with a 3-up grid.
Command: /impeccable shape

### [P2] Fake 2.4-second preloader in front of a CV
#pre animates a Math.random() percentage over fully-parsed content; START=1.85 delays the h1. ~3s before the headline is readable.
Why: 5% of a median recruiter session on theatre, contradicting principle C ("honest test gates — false assurance is worse than none").
Fix: delete #pre, set START=0.15, keep the line reveal. If non-negotiable, cap at 600ms off document.fonts.ready.
Command: /impeccable optimize

### [P2] Uncaught ReferenceError on every self-hosted load
`ReferenceError: claude is not defined` at :889. claude.use('downloads') evaluates unguarded; trailing .catch() cannot catch a synchronous ReferenceError. Related: the 327KB base64 PDF is 89% of the 366KB file.
Why: this audience opens devtools. Red console on a page arguing "no silent failure — diagnosable errors."
Fix: guard with `if (typeof claude === 'undefined' || !claude.use) return;`. Strip #cvdata for self-hosted builds — the download attribute already works.
Command: /impeccable harden

## Minor Observations

- No lang, meta description, OG/Twitter card, or favicon — link previews blank everywhere.
- Theme never persists; detection sniffs --ground for literal '#0b' — change the hex and the toggle silently inverts.
- css('--dot')/css('--accent') call getComputedStyle twice per frame in draw() — 120 forced style reads/sec.
- Both rAF loops run forever: no visibilitychange pause, no teardown when the hero leaves the viewport.
- .tl-row:hover{padding-left:16px} (:316) animates a layout property; transform:translateX() is free. [detector finding]
- Marquee ticker is aria-hidden and restates on-screen facts — a full band for zero information.
- .count writes textContent every frame; backgrounded tab freezes it on a false value ("$144K annual revenue" observed).
- GOOD: prefers-reduced-motion handling beats most production sites.
- GOOD: download button's Saving…/Saved/Unavailable/declined states.
- GOOD: 100svh hero, env(safe-area-inset-*) on dock and hail, .reel-note hidden on mobile.
